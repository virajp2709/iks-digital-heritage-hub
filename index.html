Bridging Past & Future

VPKBIET Indian Knowledge System Department

The Indian Knowledge System (IKS) Cell at Vidya Pratishthan's Kamalnayan Bajaj Institute of Engineering and Technology, Baramati, was founded to ignite rigorous scientific inquiry into India’s vast civilizational wisdom.

Rather than passive admiration, our undergraduate and postgraduate students analyze classical Sanskrit and regional texts with modern engineering rigor — applying Finite Element Analysis (FEA), Computational Fluid Dynamics (CFD), scanning electron microscopy, and hardware description languages to validate and adapt traditional techniques for sustainable future technologies.

  • ✦ Empirical Validation: Replicating ancient technologies in certified modern engineering laboratories.
  • ✦ Interdisciplinary Synergy: Collaboration between Civil, Mechanical, Computer, and VLSI departments.
  • ✦ Open Digital Access: Every project features a dedicated QR code for conference and classroom exhibition.
